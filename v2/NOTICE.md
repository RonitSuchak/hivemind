Hivemind v2 was designed and built by Ronit Suchak. It is an updated version of Hivemind, a project first built in one week by a team of four for EduX Hackathon UK 2026 (Cambridge EdTech Society × InCubed).

| Part | Who made it |
|---|---|
| The v2 website (`v2/frontend/`) | Ronit Suchak. Redesigned and rebuilt from scratch for v2. It uses none of the original website's code. |
| The backend (`backend/` at the top of this repository): simulation, scoring, matching, safety rules, API, tests | Ronit Suchak, with the two contributions listed below. |
| A fix so live chat keeps its safe reply when the database is out of reach, with its test, and a rebuild of the Lecturer run from its saved lessons | Mike |
| The `POST /api/suggestions` endpoint | Kien |
