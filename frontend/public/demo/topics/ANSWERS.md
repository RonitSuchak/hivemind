# Topic packs: answer key

These packs are made-up demo content for the hackathon site. To score them for real, the backend would need a topic parameter (which pack the student took) and these answer keys.

## English: apostrophes for possession (`english-apostrophes`)

| Question | Correct option | Why |
|---|---|---|
| q1 | friend | The spelling is friend (i before e here). |
| q2 | there | There means in that place. Their means belonging to them. They're means they are. |
| q3 | The dog barked at the postman. | It starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop. Postman is not a name so it needs no capital. |
| q4 | We bought apples, pears and grapes. | Commas separate the items in a list. There is no comma straight after bought and no comma around and. |
| q5 | quickly | Quickly tells us how the cat climbed, so it is an adverb. Climbed is a verb, cat and tree are nouns. |
| q6 | Only Sam passed the test. | Nobody except Sam means Sam was the only person who passed. |
| q7 | the teacher's coat | One owner, so add 's after teacher. |
| q8 | the sisters' room | Many owners ending in s, so the apostrophe goes after the s. |
| q9 | its | Its shows ownership and takes no apostrophe. It's would mean it is, and "the bird built it is nest" makes no sense. |
| q10 | It's | It's means it is, and "It is time to go home" makes sense. |
| q11 | The tree lost its leaves, and it's cold now. | Its (ownership) has no apostrophe. It's (it is) has one. The first its is ownership, the second is it is. |

## Science: balanced and unbalanced forces (`science-forces`)

| Question | Correct option | Why |
|---|---|---|
| q1 | Melting | A solid changing to a liquid is melting. |
| q2 | Grass | Producers make their own food. Grass is a plant that does this. |
| q3 | Cell wall | Plant cells have a cell wall. Animal cells do not. |
| q4 | The bulb goes out | A gap breaks the circuit, so no current flows. |
| q5 | 5 m/s | Speed = distance ÷ time = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s. |
| q6 | Carbon dioxide | Carbon dioxide has carbon and oxygen joined together. Oxygen, iron and hydrogen are elements. |
| q7 | 0 N | Equal and opposite forces are balanced, so the resultant is 0 N. |
| q8 | 35 N forwards | 60 N minus 25 N = 35 N, in the direction of the bigger force (forwards). |
| q9 | 15 N to the right | 60 N minus 45 N = 15 N, in the direction of the bigger force (right). |
| q10 | It keeps going at 20 m/s | Balanced forces mean the motion does not change. |
| q11 | 150 N backwards | 550 N minus 400 N = 150 N, in the direction of the bigger force (backwards). |

## Computing: reading 4-bit binary (`computing-binary`)

| Question | Correct option | Why |
|---|---|---|
| q1 | Keyboard | A keyboard sends information into the computer. The others send information out. |
| q2 | A picture | The .jpg ending is used for picture files. |
| q3 | A set of clear steps to solve a problem | An algorithm is a list of clear steps that solves a problem. |
| q4 | Byte, kilobyte, megabyte, gigabyte | Each unit is bigger than the one before: byte, kilobyte, megabyte, gigabyte. |
| q5 | 8 | x starts at 4, then `x * 2` makes it 8, so 8 is printed. |
| q6 | Yes, one condition is true | With OR, only one condition needs to be true. |
| q7 | 3 | The 1s are in the 2 and 1 places: 2 + 1 = 3. |
| q8 | 6 | The 1s are in the 4 and 2 places: 4 + 2 = 6. |
| q9 | 9 | The 1s are in the 8 and 1 places: 8 + 1 = 9. |
| q10 | 13 | The 1s are in the 8, 4 and 1 places: 8 + 4 + 1 = 13. |
| q11 | 1100 | 8 + 4 = 12, so the 8 and 4 places hold a 1: 1100. |
